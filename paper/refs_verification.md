# refs.bib verification log

Checked 2026-09-28. There are 33 entries, and each was checked against a registry or publisher record.
"Crossref" means the publisher-deposited record at `https://api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`.
Every DOI below also resolved through `https://doi.org/api/handles/<doi>`.
The checked fields are authors (order and spelling), title, venue, volume, issue, pages, year and DOI.
A script compared every DOI entry field by field against its Crossref or DataCite record. All entries matched, apart from the three notes marked (a), (b) and (c).

Access note: DBLP, IEEE Xplore, JSTOR, ACM DL and OpenReview (web pages) showed bot or JS challenges from this machine.
IEEE items therefore rest on IEEE's own Crossref deposits.
zbMATH Open, DataCite, arXiv, and the official proceedings or project pages were used where Crossref is silent.
