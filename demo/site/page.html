Tasks finished over time

Each line counts finished tasks; it ends when the last robot is back at its station. The vertical line follows the replay.

Mission time (makespan)

Time until every task is done and every robot is home, in the simulator's time units. Shorter is better; the percentage is how much longer than ALNS.

How to read the replay

Station of a robot type. Robots leave from it at time 0 and must return to it.
Task not started yet (open ring).
Coalition gathering: some robots have arrived and wait for the rest.
Task in progress: all needed skills are on site.
Task done (solid green).
Robot waiting at a task for its partners (ring).

The methods

Research demo accompanying a paper under anonymous review. Instances come from the public HeteroMRTA benchmark generator; the plans are the ones produced in our pre-registered test campaign, and each makespan is the simulator's own score.
