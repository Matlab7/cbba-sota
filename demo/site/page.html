Tasks finished over time

Each line counts finished tasks; it ends when the last robot is back at its station. The vertical line follows the replay.

Mission time (makespan)

Time until every task is done and every robot is home, in the simulator's time units. Shorter is better; the percentage is how much longer than ALNS.

How to read the replay

Station of a robot type. Robots leave from it at time 0 and must return to it.
Task not started yet (open ring).
Skills a task needs, below it: a doubled square needs two robots with that skill. A square turns orange once the robots that have arrived bring that skill. On the 500-task map, point at a task to see them.
Where a moving robot is heading: its next task, or its station on the way back.
Coalition gathering: one ring segment per robot of the coalition, orange once it has arrived (here 1 of 3).
Task in progress: green is the work done, orange the work left.
Task done (solid green; a ring pulses when it finishes). Point at or tap any task for its details.
Robot waiting at a task for its partners (orange ring). Robots point where they drive; a coalition gathers in a small circle around its task, facing it.

The methods

Research demo accompanying a paper under anonymous review. Instances come from the public HeteroMRTA benchmark generator; the plans are the ones produced in our pre-registered test campaign, and each makespan is the simulator's own score.
