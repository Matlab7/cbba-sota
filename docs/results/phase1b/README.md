# Phase 1b results

- `report_c1.txt`, `summary_c1.csv`, `summary_c1_ratios.csv`: the Phase 1 dev comparison (ALNS v1, 5 of the 8 H1 settings) re-analysed under the C1 claim of `docs/prereg-phase1.md`: each competitor separately, one-sided paired tests on log ratios, Holm correction. Dev is the tuning split, so these ratios are optimistic.
- Headroom and anytime study on the fresh validation split (`docs/headroom-2026-09.md`), regenerated by `headroom.sh` from runs/bks and runs/anytime:
  - `bks_val.txt`, `bks_val.csv`, `bks_val_runs.csv`: best-known solution per val instance (portfolio of long ALNS v2 and CP-SAT runs plus every anytime run), who found it, certified CP-SAT lower bounds (20-task settings), and per BKS run type the gap to the BKS and its own improvement in the second half of the run.
  - `bks_val_plans.json.gz`: the BKS plans (0-based routes per agent), makespans (env replay) and instance fingerprints.
  - `anytime_val.txt`, `anytime_gaps_val.csv`, `anytime_ratios_val.csv`, `anytime_val.png`, `anytime_val.md`: gap to BKS of ALNS v2, ALNS v1, CP-SAT LNS, RL(s.N) and constructor restarts at 0.5 s to 2 x B1 on 1 and 8 cores; share of the constructor-to-BKS gap closed; paired ratios (bootstrap CI, wins-losses, Holm).
  - `anytime_val_all.txt`, `anytime_val_all.md`: the same report keeping the rows disturbed by host throttling (sensitivity check).
